## CASE 1: Inpatient - COPD Exacerbation with Respiratory Failure

### Patient Information
- **Age/Sex**: 68-year-old male
- **Admission Date**: 11/15/2024
- **Discharge Date**: 11/20/2024
- **Length of Stay**: 5 days

### History of Present Illness
Patient is a 68-year-old male with a long history of COPD, presenting to the ED via EMS with acute shortness of breath that started this morning. Patient states he has had increasing dyspnea, productive cough with yellow sputum, and wheezing for the past 3 days. He has been using his rescue inhaler every 2 hours without relief. This morning he developed severe shortness of breath and called 911.

### Past Medical History
- COPD on home oxygen 2L continuous
- Hypertension
- Type 2 diabetes mellitus
- Former smoker (quit 5 years ago, 50 pack-year history)

### Physical Examination
- **VS**: BP 145/88, HR 110, RR 28, SpO2 85% on room air, Temp 38.2°C
- **General**: Elderly male in moderate respiratory distress, using accessory muscles
- **Lungs**: Diffuse expiratory wheezes bilaterally, decreased breath sounds at bases
- **Heart**: Tachycardic, regular rhythm
- **Extremities**: No edema

### Diagnostic Studies
- **ABG**: pH 7.32, pCO2 55, pO2 58 (indicating acute hypercapnic respiratory failure)
- **Chest X-ray**: Hyperinflation, flattened diaphragms, no infiltrates
- **CBC**: WBC 14,500
- **BMP**: Within normal limits

### Hospital Course
Patient was admitted to ICU for management of acute hypercapnic respiratory failure due to COPD exacerbation. Treated with:
- BiPAP for respiratory support
- IV methylprednisolone
- Nebulized bronchodilators q4h
- IV antibiotics (azithromycin)
- Continued home diabetes and hypertension medications

Patient improved over 3 days, weaned off BiPAP, transferred to med/surg floor on day 4. Discharged on day 5 in stable condition on home oxygen increased to 3L continuous.

### Final Diagnoses
1. Acute hypercapnic respiratory failure
2. Acute exacerbation of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease
3. Type 2 diabetes mellitus
4. Hypertension

### CODING ASSIGNMENT

**Principal Diagnosis**: ________________

**Additional Diagnoses**:
1. ________________
2. ________________
3. ________________

**CPT Procedures** (if applicable): ________________

---

### ANSWER KEY - CASE 1

**Principal Diagnosis**: J96.02 - Acute hypercapnic respiratory failure with hypoxia
*Rationale: Respiratory failure was the reason for admission and required intensive treatment. This takes precedence as principal diagnosis.*

**Additional Diagnoses**:
1. J44.1 - Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease with (acute) exacerbation
   *Rationale: COPD exacerbation documented and treated*
2. E11.9 - Type 2 diabetes mellitus without complications
   *Rationale: Existing condition, continued treatment*
3. I10 - Essential (primary) hypertension
   *Rationale: Existing condition, continued treatment*

**Notes**:
- Respiratory failure sequenced first because it was the main reason for ICU admission
- COPD exacerbation sequenced second as it caused the respiratory failure
- Do NOT code fever separately (R50.9) - integral to infection/exacerbation
- Do NOT code tachycardia separately (R00.0) - secondary to respiratory distress

## CASE 2: Outpatient - New Patient Office Visit for Diabetes

### Patient Information
- **Age/Sex**: 52-year-old female
- **Visit Date**: 11/15/2024
- **Visit Type**: New patient office visit

### Chief Complaint
"I'm here to establish care and discuss my diabetes."

### History of Present Illness
Patient is a 52-year-old female with Type 2 diabetes diagnosed 5 years ago. She has not been followed regularly. She reports polyuria, polydipsia, and occasional blurred vision. Home glucose readings range from 180-250 mg/dL fasting. She states her feet "feel numb and tingly" at night.

### Past Medical History
- Type 2 diabetes mellitus x 5 years
- Hypertension x 10 years
- Hyperlipidemia
- Obesity (BMI 34)

### Medications
- Metformin 1000mg BID
- Lisinopril 20mg daily
- Atorvastatin 40mg daily

### Physical Examination
- **VS**: BP 138/86, HR 78, BMI 34, Weight 220 lbs
- **HEENT**: Diabetic retinopathy noted on fundoscopic exam, no hemorrhages
- **CV**: RRR, no murmurs
- **Extremities**: Decreased sensation to light touch and monofilament bilaterally in stocking distribution. No ulcers. Pedal pulses 2+.

### Diagnostic Studies Ordered
- HbA1c (resulted: 9.2%)
- Comprehensive metabolic panel
- Lipid panel
- Urine microalbumin/creatinine ratio (resulted: 350 mg/g, indicating diabetic kidney disease)

### Assessment and Plan
1. **Type 2 diabetes with multiple complications**
   - Poor glycemic control (HbA1c 9.2%)
   - Diabetic retinopathy (background, non-proliferative)
   - Diabetic peripheral neuropathy
   - Diabetic chronic kidney disease, stage 2
   - Adjust medications, add GLP-1 agonist
   - Diabetic education referral
   - Ophthalmology referral

2. **Hypertension** - Controlled, continue lisinopril

3. **Hyperlipidemia** - Recheck fasting lipids

4. **Obesity** - Discuss diet and exercise

Return visit in 3 months.

### CODING ASSIGNMENT

**First-Listed Diagnosis**: ________________

**Additional Diagnoses**:
1. ________________
2. ________________
3. ________________
4. ________________
5. ________________

**E&M Code**: ________________ (justify level)

---

### ANSWER KEY - CASE 2

**First-Listed Diagnosis**: E11.22 - Type 2 diabetes mellitus with diabetic chronic kidney disease
*Rationale: Primary reason for visit was diabetes management; CKD is most significant complication*

**Additional Diagnoses**:
1. N18.2 - Chronic kidney disease, stage 2 (GFR 60-89)
   *Rationale: Required additional code with E11.22 to specify CKD stage*
2. E11.319 - Type 2 diabetes mellitus with unspecified diabetic retinopathy without macular edema
   *Rationale: Background diabetic retinopathy documented*
3. E11.40 - Type 2 diabetes mellitus with diabetic neuropathy, unspecified
   *Rationale: Diabetic peripheral neuropathy documented (sensory loss)*
4. I10 - Essential (primary) hypertension
   *Rationale: Documented condition, medications continued*
5. E78.5 - Hyperlipidemia, unspecified
   *Rationale: Documented condition, medications continued*
6. E66.9 - Obesity, unspecified
   *Rationale: BMI 34 documented, addressed in plan*

**E&M Code**: 99204 or 99205 (New patient, 45-75 minutes)
- **History**: Comprehensive (complete HPI, complete ROS, complete PFSH)
- **Examination**: Detailed to Comprehensive
- **MDM**: Moderate to High complexity (multiple diagnoses, extensive testing, medication adjustments)

**Notes**:
- Code all documented diabetes complications
- For diabetes with CKD, you must use both E11.22 AND N18.x
- Polyuria/polydipsia/blurred vision are integral to uncontrolled diabetes - don't code separately
- E&M level justified by new patient comprehensive history and moderate-high complexity

## CASE 3: Emergency Department - Chest Pain

### Patient Information
- **Age/Sex**: 58-year-old male
- **ED Arrival**: 11/15/2024, 14:30
- **Chief Complaint**: Chest pain

### History of Present Illness
58-year-old male with history of hypertension and hyperlipidemia presents to ED with acute onset chest pain that started while mowing the lawn approximately 2 hours ago. Pain is substernal, pressure-like, 7/10 severity, radiating to left arm and jaw. Associated with diaphoresis and nausea. No relief with rest. Denies shortness of breath. Wife brought him to ED immediately.

### Past Medical History
- Hypertension
- Hyperlipidemia
- Former smoker (quit 10 years ago)
- Father died of MI at age 62

### Physical Examination
- **VS**: BP 155/92, HR 98, RR 18, SpO2 98% RA, Temp 37.0°C
- **General**: Anxious, diaphoretic
- **Chest**: Clear to auscultation bilaterally
- **CV**: Regular rate, no murmurs, S1/S2 normal
- **Extremities**: No edema

### Diagnostic Studies
- **EKG**: ST segment elevation 3mm in leads V1-V4 (anterior wall)
- **Troponin I**: Elevated at 4.5 ng/mL (normal <0.04)
- **Chest X-ray**: Normal heart size, clear lungs

### ED Course
Patient diagnosed with acute ST elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI) anterior wall. Cardiology consulted immediately. Given:
- Aspirin 325mg PO
- Clopidogrel loading dose
- Heparin IV
- Morphine for pain
- Emergency cardiac catheterization arranged
- Admitted to CCU for emergent cardiac cath

Patient transferred to cath lab within 45 minutes of arrival (door-to-balloon time).

### Final ED Diagnosis
Acute ST elevation myocardial infarction, anterior wall

### CODING ASSIGNMENT

**ED Principal Diagnosis**: ________________

**Additional Diagnoses**:
1. ________________
2. ________________

**ED E&M Code**: ________________

---

### ANSWER KEY - CASE 3

**ED Principal Diagnosis**: I21.09 - ST elevation (STEMI) myocardial infarction involving other coronary artery of anterior wall

*Rationale: This is the definitive diagnosis after EKG and troponin confirmation. This is a life-threatening emergency requiring immediate intervention.*

**Additional Diagnoses**:
1. I10 - Essential (primary) hypertension
   *Rationale: Known condition, likely contributing factor*
2. E78.5 - Hyperlipidemia, unspecified
   *Rationale: Known cardiovascular risk factor*

**ED E&M Code**: 99285 - Emergency department visit, high severity (or 99291 if >30 min critical care documented)

**Notes**:
- DO NOT code chest pain (R07.9) separately - it is integral to the MI diagnosis
- DO NOT code diaphoresis or nausea separately - symptoms of acute MI
- EKG changes and elevated troponin confirm acute STEMI
- Specific location (anterior wall) must be coded from V1-V4 leads
- I21.09 is used for anterior wall STEMI (other coronary artery of anterior wall)
- This is initial encounter for acute MI (within 4 weeks of occurrence)

## CASE 4: Inpatient Surgery - Hip Fracture

### Patient Information
- **Age/Sex**: 82-year-old female
- **Admission Date**: 11/15/2024
- **Surgery Date**: 11/16/2024
- **Discharge Date**: 11/20/2024

### History of Present Illness
82-year-old female who fell at home yesterday while getting up from chair. Unable to bear weight on right leg. EMS called, brought to ED. X-ray showed displaced fracture of right femoral neck. Patient admitted for surgical repair.

### Past Medical History
- Osteoporosis
- Type 2 diabetes mellitus
- Atrial fibrillation on warfarin
- Hypertension
- Mild dementia

### Injury Details
- **Mechanism**: Fall from standing position at home
- **Activity**: Rising from chair
- **Place**: Home, living room

### Physical Examination
- Right leg shortened and externally rotated
- Pain with any movement of right hip
- Neurovascularly intact distally

### Diagnostic Studies
- **X-ray Right Hip**: Displaced femoral neck fracture, right side
- **EKG**: Atrial fibrillation, rate controlled
- **Labs**: INR 2.8 (warfarin held)

### Operative Report
**Procedure**: Open reduction internal fixation (ORIF) right femoral neck fracture
**Anesthesia**: General
**Details**: Patient taken to OR on hospital day 2 after INR correction. Standard surgical approach. Fracture reduced anatomically and fixed with cannulated screws. Estimated blood loss 200mL. Patient tolerated procedure well.

### Hospital Course
Post-op recovery uncomplicated. Physical therapy consulted. Patient mobilized with walker, weight-bearing as tolerated. Pain controlled. Warfarin restarted POD#2. Discharged to skilled nursing facility on POD#4 for continued rehab.

### Discharge Diagnoses
1. Displaced fracture of right femoral neck, initial encounter
2. Type 2 diabetes mellitus
3. Atrial fibrillation
4. Osteoporosis
5. Hypertension

### CODING ASSIGNMENT

**Principal Diagnosis**: ________________

**Additional Diagnoses**:
1. ________________
2. ________________
3. ________________
4. ________________

**External Cause Codes**:
1. ________________ (How injury occurred)
2. ________________ (Place of occurrence)
3. ________________ (Activity)

**ICD-10-PCS Procedure Code** (if doing CCS): ________________

---

### ANSWER KEY - CASE 4

**Principal Diagnosis**: S72.041A - Displaced fracture of base of neck of right femur, initial encounter for closed fracture

*Rationale: This is the condition established after study (X-ray) to be chiefly responsible for the admission. Requires surgical treatment.*

**Additional Diagnoses**:
1. M80.08XA - Age-related osteoporosis with current pathological fracture, vertebra, initial encounter for fracture
   *Rationale: Underlying condition contributing to fracture*
2. E11.9 - Type 2 diabetes mellitus without complications
   *Rationale: Existing condition requiring continued management*
3. I48.91 - Unspecified atrial fibrillation
   *Rationale: Existing condition, warfarin management critical perioperatively*
4. I10 - Essential (primary) hypertension
   *Rationale: Existing condition*

**External Cause Codes**:
1. W19.xxxA - Unspecified fall, initial encounter
   *OR W07.xxxA - Fall from chair, initial encounter (more specific)*
2. Y92.003 - Living room of unspecified non-institutional (private) residence as place of occurrence
3. Y93.E9 - Other activity involving personal hygiene and interior property and clothing maintenance
   *OR Y93.84 - Activity, resting, sitting*

**ICD-10-PCS Procedure Code** (for CCS exam):
0QS604Z - Reposition right upper femur with internal fixation device, open approach

**Notes**:
- 7th character A = initial encounter for closed fracture
- Must code external causes for all injuries per guidelines
- Fracture is "displaced" (per radiology) and requires specific code S72.041 (base of femoral neck, right)
- Osteoporosis with pathological fracture is important to capture
- Don't separately code pain (M79.6) - integral to fracture
- ICD-10-PCS uses different coding structure (Body System-Root Operation-Body Part-Approach-Device-Qualifier)

## CASE 5: Outpatient Procedure - Colonoscopy

### Patient Information
- **Age/Sex**: 62-year-old male
- **Procedure Date**: 11/15/2024
- **Procedure**: Screening colonoscopy

### Indication
Routine screening colonoscopy. Patient is 62 years old, average risk for colorectal cancer. No family history of colorectal cancer or polyps. No GI symptoms.

### Past Medical History
- Hypertension
- Hyperlipidemia
- Well-controlled

### Procedure Note
**Procedure**: Colonoscopy
**Anesthesia**: Monitored anesthesia care (MAC) with propofol
**Findings**:
- Prep: Excellent
- Cecum reached and identified by visualization of ileocecal valve and appendiceal orifice
- Examination of entire colon and rectum performed
- THREE polyps identified:
  1. 8mm sessile polyp in ascending colon - removed with hot snare polypectomy, retrieved
  2. 5mm sessile polyp in transverse colon - removed with cold snare polypectomy, retrieved
  3. 3mm diminutive polyp in sigmoid colon - removed with cold forceps biopsy, retrieved
- All polyps sent to pathology
- No bleeding, no complications
- Patient tolerated procedure well

### Pathology Report
- Specimen A (ascending colon): Tubular adenoma
- Specimen B (transverse colon): Hyperplastic polyp
- Specimen C (sigmoid colon): Hyperplastic polyp

### Post-Procedure Diagnosis
1. Personal history of colonic polyps (Z86.010) - after pathology results
2. Tubular adenoma of ascending colon
3. Hyperplastic polyps of colon
4. Hypertension
5. Hyperlipidemia

### CODING ASSIGNMENT

**First-Listed Diagnosis**: ________________

**Additional Diagnoses**:
1. ________________
2. ________________

**CPT Procedure Code(s)**: ________________

**Anesthesia Code** (if applicable): ________________

---

### ANSWER KEY - CASE 5

**First-Listed Diagnosis**: Z12.11 - Encounter for screening for malignant neoplasm of colon

*Rationale: This was a SCREENING colonoscopy (not diagnostic). Even though polyps were found and removed, the screening Z code is still primary because that was the original reason for the procedure.*

**Additional Diagnoses**:
1. D12.2 - Benign neoplasm of ascending colon
   *Rationale: Tubular adenoma is a benign neoplasm; code the site(s) where found*
2. K63.5 - Polyp of colon
   *Rationale: Hyperplastic polyps are non-neoplastic; use K63.5*

**CPT Procedure Code(s)**:
- **45385** - Colonoscopy, flexible; with removal of tumor(s), polyp(s), or other lesion(s) by snare technique

**OR** (more specific coding):
- **45385** - Snare polypectomy (for the 8mm and 5mm polyps removed by snare)
- **45380-59** - Colonoscopy with biopsy (for the 3mm polyp removed with biopsy forceps) with modifier -59 for distinct procedure

**Anesthesia Code**:
- **00810** - Anesthesia for lower intestinal endoscopic procedures

**Notes**:
- Z12.11 stays as primary even though polyps found (per guidelines)
- Do NOT use Z86.010 at time of colonoscopy - that's for FUTURE encounters after polyps have been removed
- Tubular adenoma = precancerous = benign neoplasm (D12.x)
- Hyperplastic polyps = non-neoplastic = K63.5
- CPT 45385 covers polyp removal by snare (can report once even for multiple snare removals)
- If different techniques used (snare AND forceps), may code both with modifier
- ICD-10 code for finding determines if this stays "screening" vs becomes "surveillance"
