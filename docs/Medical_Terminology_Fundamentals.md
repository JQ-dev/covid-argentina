## PART 1: Word Building - Prefixes, Suffixes, and Root Words

### Understanding Medical Word Construction

Medical terms are built from three main components:
- **Prefix**: Beginning of word (modifies meaning)
- **Root**: Core of word (main meaning)
- **Suffix**: End of word (describes condition, procedure, or disease)

**Example**: **hypo/glyc/emia**
- **hypo-** (prefix) = below, deficient
- **glyc** (root) = sugar, glucose
- **-emia** (suffix) = blood condition
- **Meaning**: Low blood sugar

## PART 5: Practical Application for Medical Coding

### Decoding Medical Terms - Step-by-Step

**Example 1: Cholecystectomy**
- **chole-** = bile
- **cyst-** = bladder (gallbladder)
- **-ectomy** = surgical removal
- **Meaning**: Surgical removal of the gallbladder
- **ICD-10-PCS Code**: 0FT44ZZ

**Example 2: Bradycardia**
- **brady-** = slow
- **cardi-** = heart
- **-ia** = condition
- **Meaning**: Slow heart rate
- **ICD-10-CM Code**: R00.1

**Example 3: Hyperglycemia**
- **hyper-** = excessive, above
- **glyc-** = sugar, glucose
- **-emia** = blood condition
- **Meaning**: High blood sugar
- **ICD-10-CM Code**: R73.9 (if not diabetic), E11.65 (if Type 2 diabetic)

**Example 4: Pneumonitis**
- **pneumon-** = lung
- **-itis** = inflammation
- **Meaning**: Lung inflammation
- **ICD-10-CM Code**: J18.9

**Example 5: Nephropathy**
- **nephr/o-** = kidney
- **-pathy** = disease
- **Meaning**: Kidney disease
- **ICD-10-CM Code**: N28.9 (or E11.21 if diabetic nephropathy)

## Study Tips for Medical Terminology

1. ✅ **Learn word parts, not whole words** - Master prefixes, roots, suffixes
2. ✅ **Use flashcards** - Front: medical term, Back: breakdown + meaning
3. ✅ **Practice daily** - Decode 10-20 medical terms per day
4. ✅ **Read medical records** - Apply terminology in context
5. ✅ **Use mnemonics** - Create memory aids
6. ✅ **Group by system** - Study cardiovascular terms together, etc.
7. ✅ **Link to coding** - Connect terms to ICD-10-CM/CPT codes
8. ✅ **Quiz yourself** - Regular self-testing improves retention
